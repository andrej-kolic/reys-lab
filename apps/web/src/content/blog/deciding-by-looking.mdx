Every remaining design question was a perceptual one, and perceptual questions do
not survive being argued in text. So the candidates got a route, the route got
deployed, and the answer took about ten minutes on a phone.

The method turned out to matter more than the verdict: design a comparison so that
**one thing moves**. The first two rounds changed the sans and the mono at the same
time and therefore answered neither question.

The record of that, with the losing candidates still rendering, is
[experiment 01](/lab/experiments/01-choosing-a-palette). This post is the story;
the experiment holds the evidence.
